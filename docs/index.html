Get started

  • Desktop Chrome, or a Chromium-based browser. Extensions don't run on phones.
  • Add to Chrome, open Canvas, and let Settings detect your school's Canvas. There is no Kandue account to create.
  • Quizzes, summaries and translations need desktop Chrome 138+ with about 22GB free disk, and 16GB of RAM or a GPU with more than 4GB of video memory. Deadlines work on any Chrome.

Common questions

Does it work with my school's Canvas?

On a .edu address or instructure.com, automatically. On your school's own domain, enter it in Settings and allow that one site. It uses the login you already have: no password, no API key.

Where does my coursework go?

It is read from your school's Canvas into your browser and stays there. There is no account and no Kandue server, and the AI runs inside Chrome on your computer. See the privacy policy.

Does it change anything on Canvas?

Not unless you turn on write-back in Settings. Then it writes one thing: an assignment's done state, which it keeps in step with your Canvas planner. It never submits work.

Is the practice quiz cheating?

It is built for studying: it quizzes you on a syllabus, an assignment description or the page you're reading, and never fetches a Canvas quiz's questions. Your course's rules on outside tools still apply.

Can my school or instructor tell I'm using it?

It reads Canvas with the same kind of requests your browser makes when you use the site, and reports nothing to anyone. With write-back on, the done marks it writes appear on your own Canvas planner.

Canvas has its own AI study tools. How is this different?

Instructure's AI flashcards and quizzes come only with its top Canvas tier, which your school either bought or didn't. Kandue's quizzes work on any tier, are written on your own computer, and are free.

Why don't I see the quiz or summarize buttons?

Your Chrome can't run its built-in AI (requirements under Get started). Kandue hides the buttons rather than showing ones that fail, and Settings says why.

Is it free?

Yes, with no paid tier and no ads. There is no server to run and no inference to pay for.

Kandue for Graders

For TAs and instructors on Canvas
Add to Chrome

Canvas counts the ungraded. It doesn't say which ones are yours.

A course-wide "22 need grading" says nothing about which are yours, or who has waited eleven days. Kandue for Graders lists every assignment with students of yours still to grade, longest wait first, docked beside SpeedGrader. It only reads: it sends no grade, comment or message.

Who is waiting on you

How long the first of your students has waited on each assignment, and how many are still to grade.

The worklist for your block

To grade, graded with the score, resubmitted, late, missing or excused, each student one click from SpeedGrader.

Who grades whom, read back

The split every TA is actually using, checked against every grade already entered, with the disagreements named.

Captures use a fictional course and invented students.

Get started

  • Desktop Chrome, or a Chromium-based browser, and a TA or teacher enrollment in the course. Courses you are taking don't show up.
  • Add to Chrome, then open the panel from the toolbar button or the Kandue Graders item in Canvas's left menu. In Settings, choose the course and check which students it puts in your block.
  • The toolbar badge counts your block's submissions still to grade, updated every half hour.

Common questions

Can it enter a grade by mistake?

No. Every request it makes is a read, so the worst it can show is a stale queue. Grading happens in SpeedGrader, under your course's posting policy.

Do the other TAs have to install it?

No. Who grades whom comes from a rule you set over the roster, Canvas groups named for the TAs, or the grades already entered. Nobody else has to change anything.

What if it gets my block wrong?

It checks the split against every grade already entered and shows how many agree, so a wrong block shows up as a disagreement, not a short queue. Change the split in Settings.

I'm the instructor, not a TA. Does it work?

Yes. With one grader on the course, your block is the whole class.

Where does the course data go?

Into the panel, with the last reading kept on your device so the queue shows at once. There is no account and no Kandue server. See the privacy policy for graders.
